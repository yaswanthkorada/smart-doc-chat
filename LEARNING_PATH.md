## 🤖 Your 3 AI Agents

### **📄 Ingestion Agent**
- **Role:** Document Processing Specialist
- **Tools:** DocumentAnalyzer, TextExtractor, DocumentChunker
- **Job:** Process uploaded documents perfectly

### **🔍 Retrieval Agent**
- **Role:** Information Retrieval Specialist
- **Tools:** QueryAnalyzer, VectorSearch
- **Job:** Find relevant information from documents

### **✍️ Generation Agent**
- **Role:** Response Generation Expert
- **Tools:** CitationFormatter, ResponseValidator
- **Job:** Create high-quality responses with citations
